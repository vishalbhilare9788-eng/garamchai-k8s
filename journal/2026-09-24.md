## Key decisions & why
| Decision | Why |
|---|---|
| systemd timer, not cron or a CronJob | Catches up on missed runs (`Persistent`), logs to the journal; a backup of the cluster must not depend on the cluster |
| Verify before the final name (`.tmp` → `.db`) | Retention only counts good snapshots; corrupt ones can't push good ones out |
| Restore with `--name/--initial-cluster/--initial-advertise-peer-urls` from etcd.yaml | A restore writes a new member list; the defaults (`default`, `localhost:2380`) wouldn't match the pod |
| `--bump-revision --mark-compacted` | Controllers remember a revision higher than the snapshot's; bumping forces them to re-list instead of trusting stale caches |
| Swap folders instead of editing hostPath | etcd.yaml stays identical to kubeadm's, so a future `kubeadm upgrade` can't silently switch back to old data |
